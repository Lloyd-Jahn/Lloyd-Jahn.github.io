Yi Jiang

I am an undergraduate student at the Global Institute of Future Technology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (class of 2024), majoring in Sustainable Energy with a minor in Artificial Intelligence.

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning and the physical sciences — AI for Science. I am particularly interested in geometric deep learning for accelerating molecular design and discovery, in building and training foundation models for science, and in self-driving laboratories and scientific agents that can close the loop between hypothesis and experiment.

I spent an exchange term at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, where I studied data science and artificial intelligence.

Outside of work, I enjoy piano, guitar, singing, photography, and table tennis.

Feel free to reach out — I am always happy to chat! 👋
